# Artifacts

Pages published from design sessions. Each link is private to the owner until shared from the page's Share menu.

| Artifact | Link | What it shows |
|----------|------|---------------|
| Drekki | https://claude.ai/artifact/3jTp94Wb3dhUbwk4ohVh6H | One page, eight tabs. Model: blocky stand-ins for all three growth stages, with controls for fur, astral, diet, biome, horns, hats and a rider, plus six preset eggs. Astral: the adult head in every eye state, how each is earned, what it controls. Diet: a looping animation per ability against a training dummy at a picked stage, the four diets, every food, the rules. Motion: walk, run, jump, fall, glide, dash, sleep and feed loops at a picked stage. Both galleries hide what the picked stage cannot do and say what is hidden, and every cell has a sound toggle (synthesized, muted to start, one cell at a time). Bloodlines: one bloodline at its four tiers, the generation tree, all nine bloodlines at pure, the six rare and four ultra rare lines with particles. Hatchery: the block empty, incubating and ready, a stable under a 5x5 roof with the 3x3 ceiling rule marked, and four pass/fail ceiling cases. Blocks: the leather dye picker, the mod's style (palette, one block per material, style rules), the functional blocks (Drekki Hatchery; Drekki Trough empty and stocked with each diet family's food, with dyeable leather rolls and diet plaques, and a scene of the line rule: 10 join, the 11th and a corner stay separate), then decor: a dye picker for Tufted Leather and optional blocks (planks, beam, tufted leather, shingles, bedding, fence; slabs) as a stable booth with fence corner posts and a ceiling of beam and shingle slabs, and a set row. UI: the mood icon over a drekki (Uneasy, Miserable; nothing when content), a Dark Mode Everywhere shade picker, and the screens after the UI remix's backpack redesign (a stitched leather top, the player's inventory vanilla at the bottom, light mode by default, following the shade): the drekki screen laid out like a Sophisticated Backpacks screen (equipment, model with vanilla hearts and food under it, details or traits, its pack, the player's inventory and hotbar, click and shift-click to move items; stage and mood pickers) and the hatchery screen (egg, checks, your drekki with model-rendered portraits, Check out, Pair, Trade; no inventory). |
